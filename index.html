What gets installed

Sales desk. Finds prospects from sources you name, qualifies each one with the reason shown, drafts first messages and follow ups into a queue. You read a thirty line morning brief and send what you approve.

Social poster. Drafts platform specific posts in your voice, in Arabic or English, and reply drafts. You post what you approve.
Chief of staff, included. Reads the other logs and names what is stuck, every morning.

What stays human, always

  • Sending messages, publishing posts, submitting forms, spending money, entering passwords.
  • Every draft sits in a queue until a person clicks.
  • Runs on your own Claude subscription, your browser and your logins. No credentials stored.
