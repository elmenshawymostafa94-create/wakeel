What gets installed

Sales desk

Finds prospects from the sources you name, qualifies each one with the reason shown, drafts first messages and follow ups into a queue.

Output: a thirty line morning brief and a queue file. You send what you approve.

SEO writer

Maps the topics your customers search for, writes one article draft per weekday in your voice, Arabic or English, and tracks the calendar.

Output: an article in your site repository, unpublished until you say so.

Chief of staff

Reads the other employees' logs every morning, names what is stuck, and keeps a register of the decisions you made and what came of them.

Output: one page that says what today is for.

What stays human, always

The employees have no Send button. Not one of them can:

  • send a message, publish a post, or submit a form
  • spend money or enter a password
  • touch your data outside the folder they were installed in

They run on your own Claude subscription, your browser and your logins. No credentials are stored. No data leaves your machine except through the tools you already use.

Yes. Each employee drafts in the language you set per channel. The brief itself is in the language you read first.

It shows up in the brief with the file it belongs in. You correct it in one line. Nothing went out, because nothing goes out without you.

No. The kit is open source. I run the same three employees on my own machine since 20 September 2026, and I install them for you in a day.
